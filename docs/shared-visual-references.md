# Shared Visual and Rendering References

This file consolidates the external repositories and documentation links shared during the visual-overhaul work.

## VFX and ability effects

- [LinearAbiltyCastingThreeJS](https://github.com/achrefelouafi/LinearAbiltyCastingThreeJS) - reference for GPU-driven ability casting, ribbons, particles, and impact effects.

## Sky and water

- [Three.js Sky Pro documentation](https://docs.threejsskypro.com/) - sky and atmospheric rendering reference.
- [Three.js Water Pro documentation](https://docs.threejswaterpro.com/) - water rendering and optical-effects reference.

## Volumetric clouds and atmosphere

- [volumetric_cloud_atmosphere_scattering](https://github.com/leoawen/volumetric_cloud_atmosphere_scattering/tree/main) - volumetric cloud density, noise, and atmosphere-scattering reference.
- [three-geospatial](https://github.com/takram-design-engineering/three-geospatial) - Takram geospatial atmosphere, cloud, temporal reconstruction, and rendering packages.

## Procedural environment references

- [procedural-clouds-threejs](https://github.com/CK42BB/procedural-clouds-threejs) - procedural-cloud guidance and examples.
- [procedural-weather-threejs](https://github.com/CK42BB/procedural-weather-threejs) - procedural-weather guidance and examples.
- [procedural-landscapes-threejs](https://github.com/CK42BB/procedural-landscapes-threejs) - procedural-landscape and terrain guidance.
