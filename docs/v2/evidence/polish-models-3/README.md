# Models polish 3: pilot realism, truck cab, thin shells

Second pass on `docs/v2/evidence/polish-models-2`. "Before" is the merged `webgpu-aaa` state (`994d2d2`).
Everything is generated by `tools/blender/build_pilot.py` and `tools/blender/build_assets.py`; no GLB was edited by hand.

## What changed

### Pilot (`build_pilot.py`)

- **Visor:** a separate, unbaked child node `pilot_visor` under `pilot_head`. Smoke tint, alpha 0.86, roughness 0.08, clearcoat 0.35, reduced base specular. The sky reflects off it and brightens toward the edges.
- **Face:** brow, cheeks, nose bridge and eyes are modelled behind the visor and show dimly through the tint.
- **Mask:** about 20% smaller (face piece 9 cm wide, standing 6 cm off the face), smaller valve, exhalation ports on the shell, thinner hose.
- **Helmet:** deeper rear, heavier edge roll, rolled lip on the visor housing, pivot bosses with screw heads, visor tracks, nape pad. Glossy paint with handling scuffs and chips to dark primer.
- **Body:** V-taper torso with a deeper chest and square shoulders, set-in sleeve seam, deltoid, flattened elbow, tapered forearm. Stronger creases at shoulder, elbow and waist.
- **Harness:** webbing 10 mm thick and lighter in colour; friction adapters are open steel frames with a centre bar; quick-release box with four lugs.
- **Life preserver:** the orange tube is replaced by a flat stowed collar in faded khaki with stitched seams, and two packed chest pouches with flaps and pull beads.
- **Materials:** matte suit (0.9), rubber mask (0.72), leather gloves (0.42), helmet paint (0.17 to 0.55 on scuffs), bare steel hardware.

### Truck family (`assets_truck.py`)

- **Cab:** rounded-corner sections, sill, pressed belt crease, roof that rolls over in three steps.
- **Windscreen:** proud frame with centre pillar, corner screws, cowl vent.
- **Doors:** dark shut line, radiused skin, pressed swage, recessed handle, lock, two barrel hinges.
- **Roof:** hatch with rim, lid, hinge, handle and six bolts.
- **Bonnet:** rolled shoulders, raised centre crease, seven raked louvres per side, rivet row.
- **Wings:** curved arch over the wheel with a crowned top, rolled lip and 3 cm thickness.
- **Bumper:** channel section with flanges, end plates and bolts.
- **Wheels:** protruding hub with eight bolts; tread blocks and sidewall ribs stay in the normal map.
- **Weathering:** dust 0.6 from the sills up, mud 0.8 on lower panels and 1.0 on the wings, sun fade on top surfaces raised from 0.07 to 0.20.
- `truck_covered` and `truck_destroyed` share the same cab, nose and chassis code.

### Thin shells (`kit.py` `Part.sheet`)

- **Bug fixed:** with `thick > 0` the back skin was placed in front of the front skin, so the two faced each other and the sheet had no real thickness. It now sits behind.
- **New `rim=True`:** closes the edge between the skins.
- Applied to the truck tilt (28 mm), wings, mud flaps, canvas scraps on the wreck, tent walls (25 mm) and door flaps, supply-dump net (35 mm), sangar roof sheet and tarp, bunker flap.
- Every ground unit was rebuilt because the sheet fix changes any model that uses a thick sheet.

### Runtime (`src/assets/materials.js`)

- A GLB material with clearcoat stays a physical node material in `upgradeGroundMaterials`. The `shadowSide = DoubleSide` fix from `webgpu-aaa` is kept.

## Verified

- Seen in game after the final build: all fifteen shots above. No console errors or warnings on the page.
- In cockpit view `pilot_head` is hidden and `pilot_visor` is its child, so the visor is hidden with it.
- The pilot's head turned toward the camera in the chase shots, so head tracking still drives `pilot_head`.
- `npm test` (265 pass) and `npm run build` pass.

## Not verified

- The other rebuilt ground units (AAA gun, SAM launcher, radar, artillery, MANPADS post, helipad, helicopter, sangar, bunker) were rebuilt after the sheet fix and not looked at again in game. Sangar and bunker use the changed roof sheet, tarp and flap.
- LOD1 files were not looked at in game this pass.
- `pilot_lod1` on hostile jets, night and storm lighting, the WebGL2 fallback, and the low and medium tiers.
- Draw order of the alpha-blended visor against the canopy glass was only seen from the chase poses above.
- GPU cost was not measured this pass. The visor adds one small alpha-blended draw per pilot.

## Open

- The pilot's torso and limbs are still lofted tubes with painted creases. They read as a suited figure at 1 m, not as sculpted cloth.
- The face is a simple form meant to be seen through the tint; it does not hold up with the visor removed.
- Truck panel rivets and bolts are small geometry on LOD0 and baked into the maps; LOD1 keeps only the baked version.
- The supply-dump net is still an opaque patterned sheet, now with thickness.
