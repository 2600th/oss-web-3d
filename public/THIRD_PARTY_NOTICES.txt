MiG-21 Bison Indian (War Thunder)
---------------------------------

The aircraft model in public/models/mig21.glb is an optimized derivative of
"MiG-21 Bison Indian (War Thunder)" by KojfDiscord, used under CC BY 4.0.

Author:  KojfDiscord - https://sketchfab.com/KojfDiscord

deduplicated, flattened, joined and welded, textures re-encoded to WebP capped

Techniques implemented from published work
------------------------------------------

The sky and aerial-perspective model in src/world/atmosphere/ is an original
implementation written from the published descriptions and coefficient tables
of Hillaire 2020 ("A Scalable and Production Ready Sky and Atmosphere Rendering
Technique") and Bruneton-Neyret precomputed atmospheric scattering. No source
code from those works, or from any implementation of them, is included here.

The same applies to the cloud lighting and terrain classification work, which
follows publicly described techniques (Nubis/Guerrilla and Frostbite cloud
rendering, geometric clipmaps and CDLOD) without reusing their code.

MiG-21 hostile livery (derivative)
----------------------------------

public/models/mig21_hostile.glb and public/models/mig21_hostile_lod1.glb are
derivatives of the same "MiG-21 Bison Indian (War Thunder)" model by
KojfDiscord (https://sketchfab.com/KojfDiscord), used under CC BY 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made: national markings removed, skin textures re-coloured in a
fictional two-tone grey scheme, geometry baked to metres with the nose toward
-Z, and (LOD1) cockpit detail decimated, small parts removed and the mesh
simplified. Pipelines: tools/blender/build-mig-variants.mjs and
tools/blender/build_mig_lod1.py. All other models in public/models/ added with
them (weapons, ground units, mig21_cockpit.glb) are original procedural work
of this project (MIT), generated by tools/blender/.

Terrain: elevation data (NASA SRTM)
-----------------------------------

public/assets/terrain/kargil-hi.stp and kargil-lo.stp are derived by
tools/terrain/build-dem.mjs from NASA Shuttle Radar Topography Mission 1
arc-second tiles N34E075 and N34E076 (NASA JPL / USGS, SRTMGL1). SRTM data is
in the public domain; no attribution is required, it is given here with
thanks. River courses, sky visibility and drainage planes in the packs are
computed from that elevation data by this project's own tool. The raw tiles
are not redistributed.

Terrain: techniques
-------------------

The terrain code is original to this project. It follows published methods:
geometry clipmaps (Losasso and Hoppe, 2004) with CDLOD-style vertex morphing
(Strugar, 2010), priority-flood depression filling (Barnes, Lehman and Mulla,
2014), D8 flow routing (O'Callaghan and Mark, 1984) and droplet hydraulic
erosion (after H. Beyer, 2015) for the detail tile.
