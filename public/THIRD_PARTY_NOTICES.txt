MiG-21 Bison Indian (War Thunder)
---------------------------------

The aircraft model in public/models/mig21.glb is an optimized derivative of
"MiG-21 Bison Indian (War Thunder)" by KojfDiscord, used under CC BY 4.0.

Author:  KojfDiscord - https://sketchfab.com/KojfDiscord
Source:  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/mig-21-bison-indian-war-thunder-eaefc619a50047a0acf4af12cd269b92
License: CC BY 4.0 - http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made: specular-glossiness converted to metallic-roughness, meshes
deduplicated, flattened, joined and welded, textures re-encoded to WebP capped
at 1024 px, and vertices reordered, quantized and Meshopt-compressed. The
pipeline is tools/optimize-model.mjs and is reproducible with
`npm run optimize:model`. The same attribution is carried inside the .glb
itself, under asset.extras.

Techniques implemented from published work
------------------------------------------

The sky and aerial-perspective model in src/world/atmosphere/ is an original
implementation written from the published descriptions and coefficient tables
of Hillaire 2020 ("A Scalable and Production Ready Sky and Atmosphere Rendering
Technique") and Bruneton-Neyret precomputed atmospheric scattering. No source
code from those works, or from any implementation of them, is included here.

The same applies to the cloud lighting and terrain classification work, which
follows publicly described techniques (Nubis/Guerrilla and Frostbite cloud
rendering, geometric clipmaps and CDLOD) without reusing their code.
